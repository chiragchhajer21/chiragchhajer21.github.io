Astro Bhavanam – Privacy Policy

IDEAAXIS SOLUTIONS · Last updated: 24 November 2026

We built Astro Bhavanam for astrologers and families who want their horoscopes kept private. This page explains, in simple words, what the app keeps, what leaves your phone and what you can do about it.

1. What you enter

Names, date, time and place of birth, gender, family details, phone number, e-mail, address, photos you pick, notes, remarks, appointments, reminders, consultation questions, follow-ups, fees and expenses, voice memos you record and drawings you make on charts. You enter these yourself to prepare horoscopes, reports, reminders and your accounts.

2. Where it stays

Horoscopes, charts and reports are made and kept on your phone. We never sell, rent or share your data, and we do not show advertisements.

2b. Your account and cloud backup

To use the app you sign in with your e-mail or mobile number and a password, or with your Google account. For your account we keep your name, e-mail, mobile number and city. Your password is never stored as it is – only a salted, one-way hash of it, so even we cannot read it.

So that your horoscopes follow you to a new phone, the app keeps a copy of your saved horoscopes and of a few app lists (appointments, practice records, family groups, academy, biodata, board pages and similar notes) in your account on our cloud database, hosted with Supabase. This copy is linked only to your account, travels over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and is used for nothing except bringing your data back when you sign in. Photos you attach to a horoscope are not uploaded.

When you choose "Save & sign out", your data is saved to your account first and then removed from that phone. The secure backup file (.astrovault) is locked with your own password (AES-256) before it leaves the app; we cannot open it and cannot recover a forgotten password.

2c. Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, e-mail address and Google account ID with the app. We use them only to create and sign in to your Astro Bhavanam account and to show your name in the app. We do not ask for access to your contacts, Gmail, Drive or any other Google data, and we do not share what Google gives us with anyone. Our use of this information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

2d. Deleting your account

To delete your account and everything stored with it (your details, cloud horoscopes and app lists), write to the e-mail address below from the e-mail on your account, or mention your registered mobile number. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by e-mail. Payment records may be kept as long as the law requires for accounts and tax.

2a. Payments

Astro Bhavanam Pro costs ₹99 per month. When you pay, the payment is handled by Razorpay (UPI, card or net banking) under Razorpay's own privacy policy; we never see your card number, bank login or UPI PIN. Only the phone's Device ID, the Razorpay order / payment reference, the amount and the plan end date are stored on our payment server, so that the plan can be unlocked and restored after reinstalling.

3. When the internet is used

Charts, panchang, calendar and reports are all calculated on the phone and work without internet. Besides signing in and the cloud backup above, the internet is used only in these cases:

  • when you press "Search online" for a place – the place name you typed goes to the OpenStreetMap place search;
  • the first time you write by hand in the note pad – the handwriting language pack is downloaded from Google once;
  • when you use the microphone button for voice typing – the words are converted by your phone's own speech service (for example Google), which may use the internet unless the offline language pack is installed.

4. Permissions

  • Location – only when you tap "current location", to fill in the nearest place. It is not saved or sent anywhere.
  • Camera – only for the Vastu AR compass and to photograph a paper horoscope. The compass picture is not saved; a photo stays on the screen only while you enter the chart.
  • Microphone – only while you record a consultation voice memo (saved on the phone with that client) or use voice typing.
  • Notifications – for the daily panchang, festivals, fasting days, holidays, appointments and reminders.

The call and WhatsApp buttons simply open your phone dialler or WhatsApp; the app does not read your contacts or messages.

5. Reports you share

PDF reports are made on the phone and carry a light Astro Bhavanam watermark. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself, for example on WhatsApp or by e-mail.

6. Your clients' details

When you save the details of clients, relatives or friends, please do it with their permission and use them only for consultation. Any horoscope, appointment or reminder can be edited or deleted at any time.

7. Children

The app is meant for adults and is not made for children under 13.

8. A word on predictions

Predictions follow traditional Jyotish texts and practice. Please treat them as guidance and not as medical, legal or financial advice. If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
