Astro Bhavanam – Refund & Cancellation Policy

IDEAAXIS SOLUTIONS · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Pro plan

Astro Bhavanam Pro is a ₹99 one-month digital plan that is switched on in the app right after the payment is confirmed.

Cancellation

The plan does not renew by itself, so there is nothing to cancel – if you do not pay again, Pro simply ends after the month.

Refunds

  • Money deducted but Pro not switched on: if the app does not unlock within 24 hours, write to us with the payment reference. We will either switch Pro on or refund the full amount.
  • Paid twice by mistake: the extra payment is refunded in full.
  • Once Pro has been switched on and used, the month already paid for is not refunded.

Approved refunds go back to the same UPI / card / bank account through Razorpay, usually within 5–7 working days.
