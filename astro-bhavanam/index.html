Astro Bhavanam

Vedic astrology (Jathagam) app for Android · English, हिन्दी, தமிழ் · by IDEAAXIS SOLUTIONS

Astro Bhavanam prepares complete horoscopes on your phone – Rasi and Navamsa charts, all 16 divisional charts, dasa–bhukti, upagrahas, porutham (marriage matching), panchangam and the Tamil calendar – and turns them into neat PDF reports you can share on WhatsApp.

What you can do

  • Make and save horoscopes for yourself, your family and your clients.
  • Check marriage matching, dasa periods, transits and daily panchangam.
  • Print or share multi-page PDF reports.
  • Teach classes on the digital board, and keep appointments and practice records.
  • Sign in on a new phone and get your saved horoscopes back.

Plans

Basic tools are free. Astro Bhavanam Pro is ₹99 per month and unlocks unlimited horoscopes and the professional tools.
